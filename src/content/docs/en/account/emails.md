Lynk sends you emails from **notifications@lynk.gr**. If you reply, your reply reaches our team at **insights@lynk.gr**. Each email comes in the language you chose in the app, otherwise in Greek.

## Account and security emails

These emails always arrive. You can't stop them, because they are about access to your account and its security.

| Email | When you get it |
|---|---|
| **Email verification** | When you create an account or ask for the verification link again. The link works for 24 hours. |
| **New password** | When you ask for a new password. The link works for 1 hour, once. |
| **Team invitation** | When the owner of an account invites you to their team. The invitation is valid for 7 days. |
| **Account deletion scheduled** | When you ask to delete your account. It has the deletion date and a link to cancel it. |
| **Account deletion cancelled** | When you cancel the deletion, in the app or with the link. |
| **Account deleted** | When the account and its data have been deleted. |
| **Data export ready** | When the data export you asked for is ready. The download link works for 30 days. |

## Notifications

You choose these emails yourself, per notification type, in **Settings** > **Notifications**, and you can stop them at any time. See [Email notifications](/en/insights/team/email-notifications/).

| Email | When you get it |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | At once, when something urgent that you send to email happens, for example an order that wasn't sent to Softone. |
| **Daily summary** | At 08:00, with the less urgent things since the last summary. |
| **Test notification** | When you press **Test** in **Settings** > **Notifications**. |
| **Category analysis ready** | When the analysis of a category you follow is finished. |

Each notification email holds only counts and a link to the app, never customer details. At the bottom it has the link **Stop these emails**: it opens a page that asks you to confirm and stops only that type of notification, or only the summary.

## If emails don't reach you

1. Check your **Spam** folder and, if you find a Lynk email there, mark it as "Not spam".
2. Add **notifications@lynk.gr** to your contacts.
3. If you are the account owner, open **Settings** > **Notifications** and look at the **Email from Lynk** card: it shows whether emails are going out, and you can send a test to your own address from there.
4. If the test doesn't arrive, write to us at **insights@lynk.gr**.
