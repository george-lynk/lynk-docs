*Describes the current version of the app.*

On a phone, the menu opens from the button at the top left.

## Menu

| Item | What it opens | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Revenue, commissions, cost and profit for the date range you pick | Always |
| **Products** | Your products with sales and margin | Always |
| **Orders** | All orders, grouped by day. See [Order list](/en/insights/orders/order-list/) | Always |
| **Hub** | The **Integrations Hub** with all connections | Always |
| **Skroutz**, **Shopflix**, **OpenCart**, **Shopify** | Each channel's page. See [Channels](/en/insights/channels/) | When **Show in sidebar** is on in the **Integrations Hub** |
| **Softone** | Connection, settings and the **Order Log**. See [Softone ERP](/en/insights/softone/) | As above. Available on the Pro plan; on other plans it shows a lock |
| **Relay** | Forwarding orders to your own URLs | As above |
| **Review Emails** | Emails that ask your customers to review their purchase | As above |
| **Costs** | The cost of each product | Always |
| **Alerting** | Notification settings | Always |
| **Data** | Managing and deleting data | Always |
| **Team** | Team members and permissions | Account owner only |
| **Settings** | Account and calculation settings | Always, at the bottom of the menu |
| **Get Help** | Opens a new email to Lynk support | Always, at the bottom of the menu |

Team members see only the pages they have permission for.

## Top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Date picker | Sets the date range for the **Overview**, **Products** and **Orders** pages |
| **Light Mode** / **Dark Mode** | Switches the app theme |
| **Switch to English** / **Αλλαγή σε Ελληνικά** | Switches the app language |

Your theme, language and the pages you hid from the menu are saved to your account. You get the same choices on every computer and phone you sign in on. Each team member has their own.

## User menu

Opens from your name at the bottom of the menu.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Account** | The **Settings** page |
| **Billing** | The plans (**Plans**) |
| **Notifications** | Notification settings |
| **Logout** | Signs you out |
