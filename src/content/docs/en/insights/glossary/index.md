*Describes the current version of the app.*

The terms we use throughout the docs, with the Greek term the Greek app shows.

| Term | What it means | In Greek |
|---|---|---|
| **VAT number (ΑΦΜ)** | The tax registration number of a customer or of your store. Some app screens show it as Α.Φ.Μ. | ΑΦΜ |
| **Tax office (ΔΟΥ)** | The tax office a business customer belongs to | ΔΟΥ |
| **Receipt** | The retail document for a private customer (B2C) | απόδειξη |
| **Invoice** | The document for a business customer (B2B) | τιμολόγιο |
| **Document** | Any sales document in Softone | παραστατικό |
| **Order document** | The document Lynk creates in Softone for each order. It is not a receipt or an invoice | παραστατικό παραγγελίας |
| **Final document** | The receipt or invoice that you or your accountant issue in Softone from the order document | τελικό παραστατικό |
| **Series** | A document numbering series in Softone, for example one for retail and one for wholesale | σειρά |
| **Retail (B2C)** | A sale to a private customer, for a receipt | λιανική |
| **Wholesale (B2B)** | A sale to a business, for an invoice | χονδρική |
| **Channel** | Where you sell: Shopify, Skroutz, Shopflix or OpenCart | κανάλι |
| **Marketplace** | A platform where you sell alongside other sellers, such as Skroutz and Shopflix | marketplace |
| **Commission** | The amount the channel keeps for each order | προμήθεια |
| **Net payout** | What's left of an order after the commission | καθαρό |
| **Margin** | Profit as a percentage of revenue | περιθώριο |
| **Net profit** | What's left after the commission and the product cost | καθαρό κέρδος |
| **Excluded order** | An order that was cancelled, rejected, expired or returned. It doesn't count in revenue | εξαιρεμένη παραγγελία |
| **Sync** | Bringing data from another system into Lynk, such as orders or costs | συγχρονισμός |
| **Push / send** | Creating an order or a document in another system, such as Softone or Shopify | αποστολή |
| **Relay** | Automatically creating marketplace orders in Shopify or OpenCart | προώθηση (relay) |
| **Webhook** | A notification a channel sends to Lynk the moment an order is placed or changes | webhook |
| **Demo / Live environment** | The test and the real installation of Softone | περιβάλλον Demo / Παραγωγής |
| **Account owner** | The user who created the account. Only they manage the team | κάτοχος λογαριασμού |
| **Team member** | A user the owner invited, with access only to the pages they're allowed | μέλος ομάδας |
| **FBS** | Fulfilled by Skroutz: orders Skroutz ships from its own warehouse | FBS |
