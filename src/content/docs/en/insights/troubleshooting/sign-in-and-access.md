## "Sign in failed. Please check your credentials"

**Cause:** the email or the password is wrong.

**Fix:**

1. Check that you typed the email you registered with or received the invitation at.
2. If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, enter your email and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the link you receive by email and set a new password. Check your spam folder too.

## "Link expired"

**Cause:** the password reset link is valid for 1 hour, or it has already been used.

**Fix:** click **Request a new link** and use the new link straight away.

## "Verify your email to continue"

**Cause:** you haven't confirmed the account's email address yet.

**Fix:** open the confirmation link we sent you. If you can't find it, click **Resend verification email** and check your spam folder too.

## Softone shows a lock

**Cause:** the Softone connection is available on the Pro plan.

**Fix:** in the user menu, open **Billing** to see the plans.

## A page is missing from the menu

**Cause:** if you're a team member, you see only the pages the account owner gave you permission for. For connections, the page may also be hidden from the menu.

**Fix:** ask the account owner to give you permission on the **Team** page. For a connection, switch on **Show in sidebar** in the **Integrations Hub**.

## If the problem continues

Click **Get Help** at the bottom of the menu, or write to us from the contact page on https://insights.lynk.gr. Never send your password.
