*Describes the new version of the app, coming soon.*

On the **Products** page you see every product with its score, its profit on each channel and the price that brings it to your margin target.

- [Target price](/en/insights/products/target-price/): the three prices on each channel, how they are worked out, where each input comes from and why a price can be missing.

Flows only suggests. It never changes your prices.
